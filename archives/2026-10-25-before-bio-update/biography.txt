Hi, I am Yanjun Chen.

I am a third-year Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, working with Prof. Craig Shultz in the Interactive Display Lab. Previously, I served as a Research Assistant at the Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences, under the guidance of Prof. Teng Han. I received my B.E. in Computer Science and Technology from Chengdu University of Information Technology, China.

My research interests lie in the area of human-computer interaction (HCI). I am currently exploring haptic feedback and novel sensing technologies, including compact actuators/sensors, wearables, and interactive interfaces.
